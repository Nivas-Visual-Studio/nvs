Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Nivas Visual Studio ("we", "us") collects through nivasvisualstudio.in, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to send us through the forms on this site:

  • Contact / inquiry form: your name, email address, project type and message.
  • Review form: your name, your review, and, optionally, a link to a photo.

If you email or call us directly, we also receive the details you share in that message or call.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and discuss your project.
  • To deliver design work you have requested.
  • To display your review on this website, if you submit one.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

3. Reviews you submit

Reviews you submit, including your name and photo link if provided, may be published on this website. If you would like your review edited or removed, contact us using the details below and we will take care of it.

4. Sharing your information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except where needed to operate this site and respond to you (for example, the email or form-handling service that delivers your message to us), or where required by law.

5. Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic technical storage or analytics tools to understand how visitors use it and to make it work properly. These do not identify you personally.

6. External links

Our site links to other platforms such as Behance, Fiverr, Instagram, LinkedIn and Pinterest. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, so please read their policies separately.

7. Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you, complete your project or maintain a published review. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the personal information you have sent us.

9. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
